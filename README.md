# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

## 1. Overview
This application ("App") is provided by Natsuki ("we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use this App.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information Collected by the App
The App itself does not collect or transmit personal information.

All ToDo data (tasks, deadlines, etc.) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any server.

---

### 2.2 Information Collected by Third-Party Services (AdMob)

This App uses **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)** to display advertisements.

AdMob may automatically collect and process the following information:

- Device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
- IP address (used to estimate approximate location)
- Device information (OS, model, language, etc.)
- App usage data (app launches, taps, ad interactions)
- Diagnostic data (crash logs, performance data)

These data are collected automatically by the SDK for:
- Advertising
- Analytics
- Fraud prevention

The collected data is transmitted to and processed by **Google LLC**.

## 3. How Information Is Used

The information collected by AdMob is used for:

- Displaying ads in the App
- Improving ad relevance and performance
- Measuring ad effectiveness
- Preventing fraud and abuse

Note: Ad personalization settings are controlled by Google and your device settings.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not share any user data ourselves.

However, data collected by AdMob is shared with:
- Google LLC
- Advertising partners within Google’s network

This is considered third-party data sharing.

You can control advertising data by:

- Opting out of ad personalization in your Google settings
